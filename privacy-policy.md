# Privacy Policy — Freestyle Manager

**Last Updated: September 21, 2026**

Freestyle Manager ("Freestyle Manager", "the Bot", "we", "us", or "our") is a Discord application designed to help Discord servers organize and manage Free Fire matches, matchmaking, teams, rankings, and related gaming activities.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Freestyle Manager may access or process, how that information is used, and how users can contact us regarding their data.

## 1. Information We Process

To provide its features, Freestyle Manager may process information made available through Discord, including:

* Discord User IDs
* Discord Server (Guild) IDs
* Discord Channel IDs
* Information about users participating in matches
* Match information
* Team assignments
* Match results
* Player ranking and RP information
* Wins and losses
* Bot configuration information required to operate the Bot

We only process information that is reasonably necessary for the Bot's functionality.

Freestyle Manager does not request or collect Discord passwords, authentication credentials, payment information, or Discord account tokens.

## 2. Message Content

Freestyle Manager uses Discord message content to recognize and process supported Bot commands.

Examples include:

* `!play`
* `!freestyle`
* `!help`

Message content is processed only when necessary to recognize supported commands and provide the Bot's matchmaking functionality.

Message content is not used for advertising, user profiling, or training artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

Freestyle Manager does not intentionally maintain a general history of users' Discord messages.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information processed by Freestyle Manager may be used to:

* Create and manage Free Fire matches
* Manage matchmaking
* Assign players to teams
* Manage waiting and match voice channels
* Manage match rooms
* Process match results
* Maintain player rankings and RP
* Maintain wins and losses
* Display leaderboards
* Prevent duplicate or conflicting matches
* Maintain server-specific Bot configuration
* Provide, maintain, and troubleshoot the Bot

Information is not used for advertising or targeted marketing.

We do not sell user information.

## 4. Data Storage

Freestyle Manager operates on a third-party hosting service in order to provide its functionality.

Information required for the Bot's operation may be processed or stored on the hosting environment, including information related to matches, player statistics, rankings, and server configuration.

We attempt to limit stored information to what is reasonably necessary for the Bot's functionality.

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade user information.

We do not intentionally share Discord user information with advertisers or data brokers.

Data may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate the Bot, such as hosting or infrastructure providers.

We may disclose information when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect the security and operation of the Bot.

## 6. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as it is reasonably necessary to provide the Bot's features, maintain match and ranking functionality, troubleshoot technical problems, or comply with applicable requirements.

When information is no longer necessary for the Bot's functionality, we may delete or remove it.

## 7. Data Deletion Requests

Users or server administrators may request deletion of information associated with Freestyle Manager.

For privacy or data deletion requests, please contact us through the official Freestyle Manager support server:

https://discord.gg/gPjwAnSNKy

When making a request, please provide enough information for us to identify the relevant data, such as the Discord User ID or Server ID.

We may need to verify a request before processing it.

## 8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by Freestyle Manager against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no online service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 9. Third-Party Services

Freestyle Manager operates through Discord and uses third-party hosting infrastructure to keep the Bot running.

The use of Discord is also subject to Discord's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

## 10. Children's Privacy

Freestyle Manager is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not intentionally request additional personal information from children beyond information necessary for the Bot's functionality.

Discord's own age requirements and policies also apply to users of Discord and Discord applications.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When changes are made, the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document will be updated.

Users are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

For privacy questions, data deletion requests, or other concerns regarding Freestyle Manager, please contact us through the official Freestyle Manager support server:
